Cycling expertise at everyone's service.

Sharing our passion for cycling,
supporting your needs

Genevavelo was born from a desire to promote cycling and share our passion and expertise. We support individuals, communities and companies in their use of the bicycle. Our expertise spans from training to event organisation, from advice to installation, maintenance and communication. Our focus areas: soft mobility, gentle physical activity, and sustainable development.

Businesses & Organisations

Training & events
for your staff

Soft mobility, workplace wellbeing and cycling culture in business – at Genevavelo, we put our expertise at the service of businesses and organisations to promote cycling among your teams. Our made-to-mesure workshops and seminars are designed to measure, to meet your goals: road safety, quality of life at work, health, team cohesion, sustainable development.

Private / group workshops

Practical and theoretical sessions led by professionals, to learn how to ride safely and enjoy cycling at any level. Ideal for introducing your teams to soft mobility.
